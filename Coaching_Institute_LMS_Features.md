# SkillUp LMS: Professional Coaching Institute Feature Set

SkillUp is a high-performance Learning Management System (LMS) designed to help coaching institutes streamline their examination process and provide students with data-driven insights to achieve academic excellence.

---

## 👨‍🏫 Teacher & Admin Features (Unified Control Center)
*Empower your faculty with automated tools to manage large batches and analyze performance with surgical precision.*

### 📋 Institutional Management
- **Class & Batch Organization**: Effortlessly segment students into dedicated classes and batches for focused academic delivery.
- **Bulk Student Enrollment**: Manage your student database with ease, assigning them to the right groups for test distribution.

### 📝 Strategic Content Management
- **Test Authoring Suite**: Create customized tests with specific time limits, descriptions, and marking schemes.
- **Smart Test Assignment**: Target tests to specific classes or batches with a single click.
- **One-Click Excel Import**: Save hundreds of man-hours by bulk-importing thousands of questions directly from Excel spreadsheets using our intelligent template.

### 📊 Power Analytics for Educators
- **Real-Time Gradebooks**: Access instantly generated reports for every student attempt without manual marking.
- **Advanced Question Analytics**: Identify "Bottleneck Questions"—detect which questions students find hardest based on average time spent and low accuracy.
- **Mistake Trend Analysis**: Discover common weak areas across the entire institute to adjust your teaching strategy.
- **Student Performance Tracking**: Deep-dive into individual student progress to provide personalized guidance.

---

## 🎓 Student Features (Gamified Learning Experience)
*Provide your students with a premium, focused environment that turns testing into a repeatable process of improvement.*

### 🚀 Smart Learning Dashboard
- **Personalized High-Level Metrics**: Students see their total attempts, average scores, and mistake counts at a glance.
- **Dynamic Global Ranking**: A competitive leaderboard that ranks students based on score, accuracy, and speed—motivating them to excel.

### ⏱️ Premium Test Experience
- **Focus-Oriented Exam Engine**: A professional, distraction-free interface for taking tests.
- **Per-Question Time Tracking**: Students see exactly where they spent too much time, helping them improve exam strategy.
- **Post-Exam Review**: Detailed walkthrough of every question with correct answers and explanations provided immediately after completion.

### 📕 The Mistake Book (Unique Feature)
- **Automated Error Logging**: The system automatically captures every incorrect answer into a personalized "Mistake Book."
- **Mastery-Based Revision**: Students can retry missed questions until they achieve mastery (consecutive correct solves).
- **Revision Flagging**: Students can bookmark specific questions during review to build a custom "flagged" list for last-minute revision.

### 📈 Historical Growth Tracking
- **Accuracy Trends**: Track accuracy percentages over time to visualize learning growth.
- **Activity Timeline**: A complete history of all tests taken, providing a sense of accomplishment and continuous progress.
